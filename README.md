# Privacy Policy for Hyperion Maritime

**Last Updated:** October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Hyperion: OIC NW & GMDSS Reviewer** ("Hyperion", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application and our automated customer support services on Facebook Messenger.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Facebook Messenger & Customer Support
When you communicate with our official Facebook Page ("Hyperion by Nhyle") via Messenger, we receive:
* **Page-Scoped ID (PSID):** A unique identifier assigned by Meta to route replies back to your conversation.
* **Public Profile Information:** Basic profile details provided by Meta (such as your name).
* **Message Content:** Inquiries, screenshots, payment receipts, or Terminal Request Codes you send to receive assistance or license keys.

### B. Hyperion Mobile Application
* **Offline-First Functionality:** Hyperion does not collect personal contact lists, location data, photos, or browsing history. Review questions and mock exams run locally on your device without transmitting exam progress to external servers.
* **Device Identifier (Hardware ID):** For offline activation via Terminal Keys, the app generates a cryptographic request string based on your device hardware identifier to lock the offline license key to your specific physical device.
* **Payment & Purchase Data:** In-app purchases are handled directly by Google Play. We do not store or process credit cards, debit cards, or e-wallet financial account credentials.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data solely for the following purposes:
* To provide automated and live customer support regarding exam review features, installation steps, and pricing.
* To generate and validate offline Terminal Activation Keys bound to your device hardware ID.
* To verify proof of purchase for subscription and offline clearance issuance.
* To maintain system security and prevent unauthorized key generation.

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

---

## 3. Third-Party Service Providers

To operate our support bot and application infrastructure, data is processed by the following services:
* **Meta Platforms, Inc. (Messenger API):** Facilitates incoming customer messages and outgoing webhook responses.
* **Cloudflare Workers:** Acts as a secure, stateless serverless proxy to process incoming webhook events. Chat messages are processed in-flight and are not stored in persistent databases.
* **Groq Cloud:** Processes user questions through language models to generate customer support responses.
* **Google Play:** Manages app distribution, updates, and billing transactions.

---

## 4. Data Retention

* **Chat Inquiries:** Messages sent through Messenger reside within Meta’s platform under your Facebook account's message history. Our Cloudflare serverless function operates statelessly and retains no database of chat logs.
* **Terminal Activation Records:** Terminal Request Codes and issued Activation Keys are retained only for the duration needed to verify active licensing and assist with technical support inquiries.

---

## 5. User Data Deletion Instructions

In compliance with Meta Platform Policies and data privacy regulations, you have the right to request the deletion of any data associated with your interactions with Hyperion.

To request data deletion:
1. **Via Facebook Messenger:** Send a message to the official page **Hyperion by Nhyle** stating: `"Please delete my data."`
2. **Via Email:** Send an email to `hyperionmaritimex@gmail.com` with the subject line `"Data Deletion Request"`, including your Facebook profile name or Terminal Request Code.

Upon receiving your request, any record linking your device request code or customer support exchange will be permanently removed within 30 days.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Hyperion is intended for merchant marine cadets, students, and licensed deck officers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
